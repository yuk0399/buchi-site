This is the support page for the iOS app "Buchi Slips Out".

## Contact

If you have a problem or a suggestion, please email us.

[buchi.support@gmail.com](mailto:buchi.support@gmail.com)

Please include:

- Your iPhone model
- Your iOS version
- The app version
- What you did, and what happened
- A screenshot, if you can

It may take a few days for us to reply.

## FAQ

### Can I move my progress to a new device, or keep it after deleting the app?

No. Your progress is stored only on your device.

### I bought the ad-free version but I still see ads. / I reinstalled the app and it went back to the free version.

Tap "Settings > Restore purchases" in the app. Purchases made with the same Apple ID will be restored.

### I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please request one at [https://reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### I want to change my ad consent.

In the UK and some other regions, you can change it in the app under "Settings > Ad privacy settings".

You can change tracking permission in your iPhone's "Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking".

### There is no sound.

Sound plays only when you clear a stage. It does not play when your iPhone is in silent mode, or when "Settings > Sound" in the app is turned off.
