This page explains what data the iOS app "Buchi Slips Out" handles. The app is provided by Yutaka Kawakami, an independent developer based in New Zealand.

## Accounts and data stored on your device

- The app has no accounts and no login.
- The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location (GPS).
- Your progress (stars, records and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the developer's server. The developer does not run any servers.
- If you delete the app, the records on your device are deleted.

## Support emails

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads (rewarded ads and interstitial ads). If you buy the ad-free version, no ads are shown.

To serve and measure ads, Google collects information such as:

- Device identifiers
- IP address
- Approximate location (estimated from the IP address)
- Records of ad views and taps

The app asks for your permission to track through iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT). The game works the same whether you allow it or not. If you do not allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

In the UK and other applicable regions, the app shows Google's consent screen the first time an ad is needed. You can choose not to consent. You can change your choice later in the app under "Settings > Ad privacy settings". This option appears only in those regions.

Ad content is limited to Google's T (Teen) rating or below.

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To tune the difficulty and improve the app, we collect records of how the game is played. For example:

- Starting, clearing or quitting a stage
- Number of moves and seconds taken to clear a stage
- Using a hint
- An ad being shown, making a purchase
- Solving the daily puzzle, sharing a result

Firebase also collects some information automatically:

- App instance ID (an identifier for each app install)
- Device model and OS version
- Approximate country or region
- How often and how long the app is opened

We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not use this data to personalize ads.

- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, it sends a report so we can find the cause:

- Details of the crash
- Device model, OS version and app version
- Crashlytics installation ID

- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

## Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your card number or other payment details.

We use RevenueCat to check and restore purchases. RevenueCat handles an anonymous ID created by the app and purchase records (product, date and time, transaction ID). Your name and email address are not shared with RevenueCat.

If you delete and reinstall the app, a new anonymous ID is created. You can get the ad-free version back with "Settings > Restore purchases" in the app.

- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## Sharing results

When you tap share, the app only opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## How data is used and where it is stored

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use the collected data for any purpose other than those described on this page.
- Data is stored on the servers of each company (Google and RevenueCat). It may be processed outside your country, for example in the United States.

## Children

This app is made for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and you find that your child's information has been sent, please contact us at [buchi.support@gmail.com](mailto:buchi.support@gmail.com).

## Deleting data and questions

Analytics and crash records are grouped under anonymous IDs. The developer cannot link them to a specific person.

If you want your data deleted or have any questions, please contact us at the email address below. Deleting the app also deletes the IDs stored on your device.
